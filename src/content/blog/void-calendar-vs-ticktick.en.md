TickTick already brings tasks and calendars together. You can capture work and reserve time for it.

Some tasks cannot begin together. A school assignment needs research before an outline, and an outline before the draft. Putting every step on a calendar does not automatically move the work through that sequence.

## Start outlining when the research is ready

You may want to schedule only the research first, then unlock outlining once the sources are ready, rather than filling the calendar with tasks that cannot yet start.

Void Calendar’s task dependency graph makes that path visible:

Research → Outline → Draft → Check citations.

![Four dependent steps of a school assignment](/blog/task-dependencies.en.svg)

*Finishing research unlocks outlining. Dependency illustration.*

Completing a prerequisite automatically unlocks the next task. You do not need to return to a long list after every checkbox to work out what comes next. The graph shows where you are and what is still waiting.

Backlinks keep related tasks and notes connected. Research findings, the teacher’s instructions, and earlier writing ideas can stay connected to later tasks. Dependencies establish the order; backlinks help you find the context needed to do the work.

## AI can break down the work and arrange the time

You know the assignment is due Friday, but may not know how long each step needs or where it fits between classes.

Void Calendar lets you choose Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Cursor CLI, Gemini CLI, Kimi CLI, WorkBuddy, Qwen Code, or GitHub Copilot. You can also connect a custom API.

Ask it to plan the assignment for completion by Friday, put research before outlining, and avoid late evenings. AI can break the goal into smaller tasks and arrange sessions around existing events, with task relationships and working notes available during execution.

## Plugins can hand work to AI

After planning, some of the work itself can be delegated.

Void Calendar’s plugin system brings those capabilities into the workspace. Hand a task to AI to break it down, or delegate parts suitable for AI to complete. It could organize reference material, extract key ideas, and produce an outline. Return to the result, make changes, and continue with the next step.
