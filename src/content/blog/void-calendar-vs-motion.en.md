What I want from AI scheduling is fairly simple: when a plan changes, I do not want to rethink the whole week.

Take IELTS preparation. Listening on Monday, speaking on Wednesday, writing on Friday. Then Wednesday gets another class, speaking runs over, and Friday also has an assignment due. The tidy plan needs attention again.

## Motion takes rescheduling seriously

Motion schedules tasks around working hours, duration, priority, and deadlines, then reschedules when the calendar changes. Its value is easy to understand if you want the system to maintain those rules. See [Motion auto-scheduling](https://www.usemotion.com/help/time-management/auto-scheduling).

A usable schedule also needs clear tasks. Does “practice speaking” mean ten minutes of shadowing or a full mock test? Without that detail, I still have to work out how to start.

## I want AI to see more than time

Void Calendar brings task context into planning. Each task has a corresponding note for the steps, materials, and difficulties from last time.

A speaking session could focus on Part 2: review previous feedback, do two timed attempts, and record expressions you could not produce. AI can break that down and reserve time. Those records can help the next planning session too.

You can also choose an installed AI client such as Claude Code or Codex, or connect a custom API. Keep the AI you are comfortable with.

## Free time and available energy are different

After three morning classes, the calendar might still have two free hours. Whether those hours suit a full mock exam is another question.

When reviewing a plan, I look for room to breathe, smaller steps, and a way to restart after an interruption. Time boxes, notes, and scheduling preferences in Void Calendar serve that practical need.

## Which one fits

Choose Motion if:

- You want the system to fill your calendar by priority and deadline and keep rescheduling as plans change.
- You need team project management or mobile apps today.
- You are happy to pay per seat for a hosted AI service.

Choose Void Calendar if:

- You use an Apple silicon Mac and want to review a plan before AI touches existing events.
- You want to use your own model and API key, with requests going straight from your Mac.
- You want a note with every task and focus records written back to the time box.

Look closely at Motion if routine rescheduling is the main burden. Explore Void Calendar if you also want working instructions, records, and your chosen AI together.
