If you already use Gmail and Google Calendar, Google Tasks is an easy place to capture a quick action.

**In short:** If your tasks are mostly small, your day already runs through Gmail and Google Calendar, and you want to jot things down on your phone, Google Tasks is light and convenient, so keep using it. Consider Void Calendar if you plan on a Mac, your tasks need steps, materials, and protected focus time, and you want AI to fit them into time that is actually free while your data stays on your own computer.

Reply to an email, submit a form, print some material. Write it down, give it a time, and check it off. That simplicity is useful.

My question is what happens when the work grows beyond a small reminder.

## Google Tasks can schedule duration too

Google Tasks supports details, subtasks, recurrence, and deadlines. Creating a task in Google Calendar also lets you set a start time and planned duration. See [Google’s task guide](https://support.google.com/tasks/answer/7675838?co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop&hl=en).

For a few straightforward tasks, that may already be enough. Another tool should solve a real problem.

## “Prepare for IELTS” still leaves a lot to decide

Listening, speaking, reading, and writing require different materials and kinds of time.

A writing session might mean Task 2 only: review the previous corrections, outline the argument, then write a timed answer. Afterward, record recurring grammar mistakes and ideas that were hard to express.

Void Calendar gives tasks corresponding notes for those steps, references, and records. AI can organize them, break down work, and plan another session. Dependencies can establish which steps need to happen first.

Opening the task should give you a starting point, rather than another vague goal.

## Keep your existing tasks in the process

Void Calendar can’t connect to Google Tasks yet; that integration is planned. Until then, the two can split the work: quick reminders stay in Google Tasks, while tasks that need steps, material, and focused time go into Void Calendar, alongside classes and your other calendars.

## Which one fits

Choose Google Tasks if:

- You need to capture tasks on Android or iPhone today.
- Your email and schedule already live in Gmail and Google Calendar.
- Most of your tasks take a step or two and don’t need their own notes.

Choose Void Calendar if:

- You plan your day on an Apple silicon Mac.
- Your tasks need steps, reference material, and a record of what happened.
- You want AI, running on your own model and API key, to place tasks into time that is actually free.
- You’d rather keep tasks, notes, and focus records on your own computer.

Keep the familiar workflow for small reminders. For me, the reason to add a tool is when a task needs more room for instructions, material, and what happens afterward.
