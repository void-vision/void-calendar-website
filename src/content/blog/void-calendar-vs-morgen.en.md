What appeals to me about Morgen is the ordinary problem it takes seriously: more than one calendar, and tasks in more than one place.

**In short:** If your calendars and to-dos are spread across Google, Outlook, Todoist, Notion, and more, and you need Windows, Linux, or mobile apps, Morgen is built to pull them together. If you use an Apple silicon Mac, keep your schedule in Apple Calendar, Reminders, Google, or iCloud, and want task notes and scheduling in one place, try Void Calendar.

## Bring scattered commitments together

Morgen connects calendars and task services across desktop and mobile platforms. Its AI Planner uses Frames to express a weekly rhythm, then proposes sessions you can preview, adjust, and accept. See [Morgen’s AI Planner guide](https://www.morgen.so/guides/plan-your-day-using-the-ai-planner).

That is appealing when your existing task tools work well and you mainly need time for the tasks. I also ask where the working material will be when the session starts.

## A class presentation needs more than an hour on a calendar

Preparing a presentation involves research, an outline, slides, and rehearsal. “Prepare presentation” is easy to capture. Doing it means finding instructions, examples, and previous feedback.

Void Calendar keeps that context close. Projects contain tasks, and each task has a corresponding note for material, steps, and working records. Open the scheduled task and see which part to begin.

Today it syncs both ways with Apple Reminders; Google Tasks, Microsoft To Do, and Todoist connections are planned. Choose your AI to break down work, reserve time, and organize the records.

## I care about what happens when work starts

Morgen plans across existing tools and lets you review AI proposals, which matters to me too. Void Calendar focuses on keeping planning and execution in one workspace. It runs on macOS today; Android, iOS, Windows, and Linux versions are planned, and each should get interactions that suit the device.

## Which one fits

Choose Morgen if:

- Your calendars span Google, Outlook, iCloud, and other accounts, and your tasks live in Todoist, Notion, or similar tools.
- You need Windows, Linux, or mobile apps today.
- You like setting a weekly rhythm with Frames, then approving the AI's plan.

Choose Void Calendar if:

- You use an Apple silicon Mac and your schedule lives in Apple Calendar, Reminders, Google, or iCloud.
- You want each task's material, steps, and working records in its own note.
- You want to schedule with your own AI model and keep events, tasks, and notes on your Mac.

Try an assignment you actually need to submit. Notice whether it is easy to start when the reserved session arrives, rather than only whether the week looks organized.
